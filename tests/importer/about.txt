`small.txt` exercises union and atom-or-cell cases, fields, collections,
recursion, and unsupported forms. `shadow.txt` checks local generator
shadowing and missing provenance. `shadow-static.txt` checks that a local
list shadow alongside a nested ordered map is rejected. Static sibling
collections and dependent sibling molds are tested separately. The synthetic
hold in +bad-branch-fixture checks that a failed Hoon branch expansion returns
an error. `tlon-epic.txt`, `tlon-presence.txt` and `tlon-docs.txt` are the
unmodified `desk/sur/epic.hoon`, `desk/sur/presence.hoon` and
`desk/sur/docs.hoon` from tloncorp/tlon-apps at commit
38f9328f4580000a37a7fdc3ab820fcd65069487; its MIT license is in
`tlon-license.txt`. The files use .txt so -test does not build them as
Hoon tests. The separately stored `tree.txt` is a diagnostic projection of the
internal type graph, not the Jibb type printer. It runs under eval and on the fake
ship; +fixture-test also checks the underlying structured values.
