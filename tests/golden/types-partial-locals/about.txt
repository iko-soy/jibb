Grammar, Modules: "Names may refer to each other in any order, and no name
may shadow one already in scope". Definitions at the start of a body are
visible to one another and its final expression. Partial inference resolves
acyclic local values, generalizing their free variables except those captured
from enclosing bindings or explicit capitals. It declines recursive groups,
duplicate names and shadowing; simple local molds are now supported. A module remains unchecked.
