tests: a definite cast mismatch inside a local `++` reports the mold and expression sites, as the same cast does at top level: in a local value, in a later sibling that an earlier one waits on, after a local gate's self-call, in a nested body, and beside a sibling that uses the untyped sample without constraining it. A later sibling that infers but is then unsupported, because it forces an explicit capital to a concrete type or leaves an uncertain type hole, reports its own unsupported message, even after an earlier mismatch: a sibling's cast of the untyped sample to `!!` makes every cast of it fit, so an earlier failure can pass on a retry. When a sibling has constrained the untyped sample before the definitions stall, a mismatch that another order accepts keeps the generic message.
