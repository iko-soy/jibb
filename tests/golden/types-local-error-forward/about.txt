tests: a local value or gate that refers forward to a later sibling still infers once that sibling does, through a chain; a reference to a sibling not yet inferred is not reported as an error. When a sibling never infers, because it names something unavailable or its tag stays pending, the waiting definition keeps the generic unsupported message; when the sibling infers but forces an explicit capital to a concrete type, its unsupported message stays.
