The checker accepts caller-supplied results from the Hoon type reader. This
fixture compiles a small /sur against zuse, selects its molds, and passes the
actual reader results to the inference fragment. It checks fixed atoms, nested
faced records, and failures that must remain unsupported until the checker can
translate them. A missing import and a duplicate supplied mold fail closed.
The types output is partial and does not claim that the module was checked.
The same fixture runs under urbit eval and through -test on the fake ship.
