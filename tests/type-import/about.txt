The checker accepts unchanged, successful results from the source-aware Hoon
reader as a trusted boundary. It independently rejects malformed graphs and
forks whose supplied cases overlap, but a graph alone cannot reveal source
cases Hoon already collapsed. This fixture compiles a small /sur against zuse,
selects its molds, and passes the actual reader results to the inference
fragment. It checks fixed atoms, valid term singletons including Hoon `%$`,
nested faced records
and unnamed unions, including matching and mismatched casts. Forged
unsupported, malformed, singleton and overlapping forks are rejected; the
reader itself rejects an overlapping source union before Hoon can collapse it.
A missing import and a duplicate supplied mold fail closed. The types output
is partial and does not claim that the module was checked. The same fixture
runs under urbit eval and through -test on the fake ship.
